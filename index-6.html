About

I write copy that sounds like a real person said it.

I'm Jenna, a copywriter. I write the words brands post and send: captions and carousels, email sequences and newsletters, product pages, scripts and launch copy.

I start with the audience. My background is in mass communication, including advertising, PR, online journalism and audience analysis, and I hold a HubSpot Social Media Marketing certification. Before I write a line, I want to know who the customer is, what stops them and what would make them act.

Most of the work above started with a brand's own caption or product page. I keep the facts, find the one idea worth leading with, and cut anything that could belong to any brand. If a line still feels off, I rewrite it until it doesn't, and I can explain every choice I make.

Cowrie Bracelet. From my "Brands I'd kill to write for" series: their copy first, then mine.

Before · ÌTURA's copy

The Cowrie Bracelet is a reminder of the unseen threads that binds and connects us, with each cowrie link representing knots of strength, resilience and culture.

After · my rewrite

Before cowries were jewelry, they were currency. Across West Africa, they moved from hand to hand as payment, dowry, proof of status. Worn by the women who could afford to wear their wealth in plain sight. That history doesn't fade just because the shell became decoration. It's still on you.

The Cowrie Bracelet carries that weight in 18k gold, each link a small, deliberate nod to a shell that once meant something in every marketplace, every bride price, every hand it passed through before it reached yours.

Wear it alone, and it holds its own. Stack it with the Cowrie Necklace, and you're wearing the fuller story, not a costume of heritage, but the real thing, made for a wrist that carries it forward.

This isn't jewelry that borrows from tradition. It's jewelry that remembers it.

Amalphine Cosmetics

Velvet Milk, a donkey milk lotion from an Italian skincare brand on the Amalfi coast.

The brief

Tell a short story about the donkey as a traditional animal of the Amalfi coast and a symbol of the region today, and connect it to the Velvet Milk donkey milk lotion.

My approach

Two angles on one idea: the donkey as heritage, and the product as a continuation of it. Version 1 speaks as the brand, with a clear before and after. Version 2 speaks in a personal voice, through a grandmother's memory, to feel lived in rather than sold. Both keep the product claim modest and tie it to place.

Version 1, the brand voice

Before the roads, there were donkeys. For generations, they carried everything up these cliffs: lemons, olive oil, life itself. Nothing else could climb where they climbed, or wait as patiently as they did.

Our Velvet Milk carries a piece of that same heritage. Donkey milk has soothed skin since Cleopatra's time, and ours is nourished by the same coast that shaped these hills.

A small tribute to the animal still watching over them.

Version 2, the personal voice

My grandmother used to say you could hear the donkeys before you saw them. Bells clinking against the cliffs, hooves finding footing where the paths hadn't been carved yet.

They don't carry lemons up the hills anymore, but they never really left. You'll still find them painted on ceramics, tucked into gift shop windows, part of what makes this coast feel like this coast.

Velvet Milk holds onto that same thread. Real donkey milk, an ingredient people on these hills have trusted for centuries, long before "skincare" was even a word.

Some things don't need reinventing. They just need remembering.

A lot of people think the more skincare products they use, the better their skin will become.

But that's not always true. Overloading your skin with too many products can actually cause breakouts and make the problems you're trying to fix worse.

Your skin doesn't need more. It needs the right things.

Tomorrow, we'll share a simple routine that actually works.

Warmly, Estrella Beauty

Email 3Your Skin is Already the Most Beautiful Accessory

A couple of years ago, one of our founders struggled with confidence because of her skin. She started hiding her face and avoiding social events, all because of breakouts and skin issues.

We know this is a struggle many of us face, and we want you to know: you're not alone. Estrella Beauty was created to help you glow, yes, but also to help you feel confident again.

We built Estrella for skin like yours and mine. Simple, intentional, and effective. Because we know simple care is enough.

Reply to this email and tell us your skin concern.

Strategy note

The sequence moves from welcome to education to emotional connection to conversion, a classic nurture arc. Email 3 does the heaviest emotional lifting to build trust before the ask in email 4. Tone stays warm throughout without becoming overly familiar.
